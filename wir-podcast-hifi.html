Questions we get asked.

01How long does recording take?
Twenty minutes of your time, including the sound check. We record remotely, Tuesday through Thursday, and work around clinic hours.
02Will I know the questions ahead of time?
Yes. All three arrive as soon as you are booked. You will also be asked whether there is anything you would prefer we not raise, and we honor that without exception.
03Do I have to pay to be interviewed?
No. The interview, the episode, the transcript, the creatives, and the promotion are all free. Your Index profile can also be free.
04What is the difference between a free and a Premium profile?
A free profile lists you on the Index and lets you nominate and rate solutions. Premium adds being featured as a named expert in the monthly Wellness Index Report, inbound leads from your profile, and the ability to collect patient and client reviews.
05What if I do not want to publicly criticize a product?
That is entirely your call. You can describe what you have moved away from without naming a brand, or keep the conversation focused on what you do use. We never push a provider to say something they are not comfortable putting their name on.
06What does it mean to nominate a company?
You can put any company or solution forward for inclusion on the Index, whether or not it is currently listed. Only providers can nominate. Companies cannot nominate themselves.
07Can I use the episode on my own channels?
Yes, and we want you to. You receive the full episode, clips, transcript, and artwork, and you are free to use all of it indefinitely.
08Who do I talk to about scheduling?
Anne Delgado, our Chief of Staff, coordinates every booking directly with you.

What SEO, AEO, and GEO actually mean for your practice

Search engine optimization means people find you when they search for what you do. Answer engine optimization and generative engine optimization mean something newer. When a patient asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google's AI overview who the credible providers are in your specialty, those systems answer using published, attributed, verifiable sources.

Being cited by name in a syndicated monthly report puts you in that source material. A social post does not.

How this stays honest

Revenue is normalized. Everyone pays the same fee to participate: experts $999 and solutions $999. No brand can buy a nomination, a rating, or a position in the Index.

Nominations and ratings are submitted independently of membership tier. A free member's rating carries exactly the same weight as a Premium member's. Premium membership buys a provider a published platform for their own expertise. It does not buy any company a better score.

Appearing on the podcast is free, requires no membership of any kind, and always will be.

Some providers want more than a platform.

The Index amplifies your expertise. If you are building a broader position in this industry, as an author, a speaker, a board advisor, or the recognized authority in your category, that is a different conversation and a longer one. I take a small number of those conversations each month.
