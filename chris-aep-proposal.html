How you’ll get found

Google shows two kinds of results. They work differently and start working at different times.

medicare agent near me

The map

When someone searches for help nearby, Google shows a short list of local businesses with a map, near the top of the page.

What Google looks at
Your Google Business Profile: how well it matches the search, how close you are to the person searching, and how well known your business is. Reviews count toward that.
Who you’re up against
Other agents near Cedar Springs.
When it starts working
After Google verifies your profile, which can take days or weeks. It grows as reviews come in.

Who does what

  1. Me Set up your Google Business Profile for Cedar Springs and nearby towns, or add Medicare to the profile Four Seasons already has. (Step 1)
  2. You Verify the profile. Google checks the business directly, often by asking the owner for a short video.
  3. Me Build the page so Google can read it: a clear page title, and your name, address and phone written exactly as they appear on your profile.
  4. Me Connect Google Search Console, Google’s free tool that shows which searches bring people to your page.
  5. You Ask your clients to leave Google reviews.
  6. Me Write articles that each answer one question people in your area ask. (Step 2)
  7. Me Report what’s working, every month you keep Essential care.

No one can pay Google for a better local ranking. Google says so itself. Anyone who guarantees you a spot on the first page is guessing.
