Before I build

Two conflicts in our own sources
  • Date. translationallongevity.com, bostonlongevityweek.com and healthspanaction.org all say October 7 to 11. bostonbiolife.com/upcoming-events says October 8 to 11, and the existing Summit board in the BBL template system says "Saturday, October 11." Two of our own assets need correcting either way.
  • Track 03 name. healthspanaction.org calls it Regenerative Medicine. translationallongevity.com calls it Translational Science. I have used Regenerative Medicine on the boards because it is the clinical framing. Confirm which is canonical.
What unblocks the rest
  • Agenda. Three program boards depend on it. Without it I hold those slots and run faculty boards instead.
  • CME. Not mentioned on either summit site. The badge stays off every Summit board until someone confirms. If it clears, it goes on all of them.
  • Speaker photo rights. 30+ headshots are published on the summit site. Confirming we can use them in social graphics unblocks ten boards.
  • Master logo file. The chip on every board here is redrawn at thumbnail scale. Production needs the real asset.
  • Who shoots the event. Seven event-week posts are floor photography. Assign one person by early September or they will not happen.
  • Which doctor amplifies, so the collab boards can be tagged correctly from post one.
