The mark

The real BBL leaf from the file you sent, on transparent, sitting straight on the field with no container behind it. The supplied artwork carries a soft white glow roughly 14 px wide baked into its edge, so a plain knockout leaves a halo on any dark background. The edge is cut inside that glow and the white matte is divided back out, which is why it now reads clean on navy, on royal and on green. A soft drop shadow does the separating that the white container used to do. The same leaf is traced to a vector outline for the oversized watermark on the banner, so it stays crisp at 4200 px.

  • TLS is also Transport Layer Security. It does not matter on a tile beside the full page name. It would matter if the acronym were ever used alone in copy. Flagging once.
